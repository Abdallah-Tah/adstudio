Rechargeable electric lice and flea vacuum comb for humans and pets. White
cylindrical handheld body with a detachable suction pin brush and a smoothing
pin comb head (fine metal teeth), a transparent detachable dust collection
chamber with washable filter screen, two front buttons (power and speed
adjustment), a digital speed display, a USB Type-C charging port, and an air
outlet. Blue interior light in the chamber when active. Effectively removes
lice, fleas, dirt and debris from hair; chamber and filter rinse clean under
water.
